Mochi Picnic Privacy Policy

Prepared November 12, 2026. Developer: DAGUGAMES. Android package: com.dagugames.mochipicnic.

This policy describes Mochi Picnic, a puzzle game intended for people aged 13 and older. We do not require a game account. Contact: gahee1577@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your progress, stars, coins, item inventory, settings, reward history, event and simulated league records, age group, advertising interval, purchase transaction identifiers and pending rewarded-ad receipts locally. We do not upload your puzzle saves or operate a player-profile server. The age group is used locally to apply advertising protections; a full birth date is not requested. The advertising SDK receives the applicable under-age treatment flag.

Advertising and privacy choices

We use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Google may collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location derived from them), device or account identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We request non-personalized ads, a general-audience maximum ad content rating, and under-age treatment for users under 18. We do not request or show ads for users who select an age below 13, or until an age group has been selected. These choices do not prevent all processing needed to deliver and measure ads.

Where required, Google presents privacy information and consent choices. You can revisit available choices in Settings > Privacy > Ad Privacy Choices. From level 11, you can choose a rewarded ad to add 5 moves after moves run out, at most twice per attempt. Buying Remove Ads stops automatic interstitial ads and provides these continues without ads. The game stores an earned-reward receipt on your device until the added moves are saved, to prevent lost or duplicate rewards. There are no banner placements.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Google Play processes payments. The game receives product details and prices, signed purchase information, purchase tokens and purchase status to verify purchases, deliver items, prevent duplicate grants and restore Remove Ads. We do not receive your card number or payment credentials. The game validates the store's signed purchase information locally and acknowledges or consumes purchases only after saving the grant. Google retains purchase records under its own policies.

Remove Ads can be restored with the same Google Play account. Consumed item packs and other local progress cannot be restored after app data is deleted. Refunds and payment issues can be handled through Google Play; contact us for game-related support.

Children and teenagers

Mochi Picnic is intended for ages 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The game asks for an age group before first requesting ads. It does not collect a full date of birth. Under-13 selections disable ads. Users under 18 receive the under-age advertising treatment described above. If you believe personal information about a child has been sent to us, contact us so that we can review and delete information we control.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and resolve the issue. We retain support correspondence while needed for the request and related obligations. You may ask us to delete correspondence we control, subject to required retention.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local game data remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. To delete it, use Android Settings > Apps > Mochi Picnic > Storage > Clear data. This permanently removes local progress and consumable balances; it does not delete Google's payment or advertising records. We cannot recover deleted local saves.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing, withdraw consent, or appeal a privacy decision. Contact us concerning information we control, and use Google's privacy controls concerning information Google controls. Regional ad privacy choices, including available opt-out controls, are provided through Google's privacy form. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Security and international processing

The app uses encrypted network connections for integrated Google services and does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Google may process data in countries outside your own under its policies. Local game data is stored in the Android app's private storage. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The date above identifies the current version.
